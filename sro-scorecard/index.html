How the score works

Each office is placed on a 0–100 percentile for six measures, compared with all 107 offices for the chosen period. For measures where lower is better, such as processing time, the percentile is flipped so that 100 is always best.

The score is the weighted average of those six percentiles. A score of 50 means the office sits in the middle of the state. Colours show distance from the middle, not pass or fail.

Built by Ayush Dewangan as a prototype of the Sub-Registrar performance analytics proposed during the CM Good Governance Fellowship. Data is regenerated by scripts/generate_data.py; replace data.js with real figures in the same shape to use it for review.
