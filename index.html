The fellowship · Aug 2025 – present

One fellow, three departments

I work with the department Secretary's office on reforms that have to survive real files, real counters and real citizens. Pick a department.

Registration & stamps reform

I catalogued 30 reforms from 10 states (13 policy, 17 technical) and benchmarked Chhattisgarh against Karnataka, Telangana, Maharashtra and Gujarat. The Secretary directed officers to implement two: Sub-Registrar performance analytics and dedicated counters for women and senior citizens.

A DPR for 107 offices

A feasibility study recommended a sequenced roadmap over an immediate blockchain ledger. I then wrote the Detailed Project Report: architecture, legal framework, risk register and a ₹3.24 crore programme across 107 Sub-Registrar offices, with an interactive proposal dashboard.

Cool Roof, at zero budget Pitched

Instead of a subsidy, I pitched a rule: heat-reflective roofing of SRI 78 or more becomes a condition of the occupancy certificate. Enforcement sits inside four building-permission checkpoints, and the ₹2–4 per sq ft cost is borne by the developer. I benchmarked it against Telangana's 2023 order.

Course work on the same department

An IIM Raipur budget analysis of the Housing & Environment Department using zero-based and flexible budgeting, and the environment and Nava Raipur command-centre module of C-Drishti, a group prototype for an enforcement-intelligence dashboard.

CM Helpline 1076

Chhattisgarh launched CM Helpline 1076 on 9 June 2026: a 24×7 line, portal, app and WhatsApp channel for citizen grievances. For Commercial Tax and Housing & Environment, I categorised and mapped the grievance services, built the L1–L4 officer escalation map, and remain the departmental point of contact for portal operations.

I was also asked by the Secretary's office to prepare guidance for operating the helpline dashboard at L4, in English and simple Hindi.
