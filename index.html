01 Commercial Tax · Registration

30 reforms in, 2 directed for implementation.

Problem
Other states had already solved problems Chhattisgarh still faced, but nobody had ranked which fixes to adopt first.
What I did
Benchmarked 10 states and catalogued 30 reforms: 13 policy, 17 technical.
Result
The Secretary directed officers to implement two: Sub-Registrar performance analytics and counters for women and senior citizens.

02 Commercial Tax · Registration

Measure offices, not the state.

Problem
A single state-wide turnaround number hides the offices where citizens actually wait.
What I did
Designed office-level performance analytics, then built a public prototype on synthetic data.
Result
Directed for implementation. Open the prototype →

03 Commercial Tax · Registration

A ₹3.24 crore DPR: clean records first, ledger last.

Problem
A ledger only proves what is written into it, so the records have to be clean first.
What I did
Ran the feasibility study, then wrote the DPR: architecture, legal framework and risk register.
Result
A sequenced programme across 107 Sub-Registrar offices: clean records first, ledger last.

04 Housing & Environment Pitched

Cool roofs, and the state pays nothing.

Problem
Heat-reflective roofs cool homes, but a subsidy would add a new cost to the state budget.
What I did
Turned it into a rule: an SRI 78+ roof becomes a condition of the occupancy certificate, checked at four points.
Result
Pitched to the department. The developer pays ₹2–4 per sq ft once; the state pays ₹0.

05 Good Governance Live since Jun 2026

CM Helpline 1076: who answers, and when it escalates.

Problem
A complaint is only as good as the officer it reaches and the deadline behind it.
What I did
Mapped grievance categories and the L1–L4 officer chain for Commercial Tax and Housing & Environment.
Result
Live statewide. I remain the departmental point of contact for portal issues.

    Is "आयुष देवांगन" the same person as "Aayush Devangan"?

    Land records in India are written in Hindi, deeds and bank files in English, and nobody spells a name the same way twice. This is where a lender's title check usually needs a human. This is a transparent matcher: every step is shown.

      Rule-based transliteration, honorific and initial handling, token-order normalisation and Jaro–Winkler similarity, running in your browser. A heuristic to show the problem, not a production model.

      The API a lender wants from a land record

      One call that answers: who owns it, is it mortgaged, did the mutation go through, and do the names match. Here is the schema I would propose. Every response is synthetic.
