experiments:
- title: Hindi-to-English name matching
  why: The weakest link in a Hindi-belt title chain is often the name, not the record. A transparent matcher tells ops which pairs need a human.
  metric: Share of real pairs auto-matched, and false matches per 1,000.
- title: One district, fully online
  why: Pick Raipur and test how much of a standard title checklist can be answered from public sources alone, without a field visit.
  metric: Checklist items answered online, before and after.
- title: Policy-change alerts
  why: A state order that changes a fee or a portal can silently break a search flow. Ops should hear about it the day it is notified.
  metric: Days between a notification and the product update.
