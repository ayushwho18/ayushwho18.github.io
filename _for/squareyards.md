experiments:
- title: Hindi deed and name accuracy
  why: Before ingesting a Hindi state at scale, measure how often names disagree across records and how much needs a human.
  metric: Auto-match rate and false matches per 1,000 pairs.
- title: ULI demand test
  why: Ask two lenders active in Chhattisgarh and Madhya Pradesh which title and valuation checks they would buy first.
  metric: Signed pilot interest before build.
- title: Guideline-value tracker
  why: State guideline values drive e-valuation and stamp duty. A tracker catches revisions the week they are notified.
  metric: Lag between a revision and the model update.
