I spend my days inside government departments, and most of what I learn there turns into files that few people read. This site is where I will publish what I can share: analysis built from public sources, prototypes on synthetic data, and short notes on news in public policy and property data.

<!--more-->

Three kinds of posts will appear here:

- **News:** short takes on developments worth following, with a link to the source.
- **Analysis:** longer pieces with data and sources, like the [ten-state registry scorecard](/registries/).
- **Notes:** shorter thoughts and updates.

Everything here is my own view and does not represent the Government of Chhattisgarh. Nothing on this site comes from departmental files.
