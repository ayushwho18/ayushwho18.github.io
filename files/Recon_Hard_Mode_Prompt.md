You are the accounts reviewer for a building contractor (fictional). Work through the task below and show your working. Finish with a clearly labelled final answer.

## Rules
1. Prepare the bank reconciliation for the month ended 31 October 2026 and find the adjusted balance. All amounts are in INR. All parties and documents are fictional.
2. Cheques issued and recorded in the cash book but not presented at the bank by month end are outstanding cheques. Deduct them from the bank balance, unless they are stale.
3. A cheque is stale 90 days after the date on it. A stale cheque is not outstanding: reverse it in the cash book (add it back to the cash balance).
4. Receipts recorded in the cash book but not credited by the bank at month end are deposits in transit. Add them to the bank balance.
5. Items the bank has recorded that the cash book has not (charges, interest, direct debits, direct credits, returned cheques) are adjustments to the cash book.
6. A returned (dishonoured) customer cheque reverses the original receipt: deduct it in the cash book.
7. Errors in the cash book are corrected in the cash book. Errors made by the bank are reconciling items on the bank side (to be claimed from the bank).
8. After all adjustments, the adjusted cash book balance must equal the adjusted bank balance.
